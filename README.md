# PoleSim Gear

Version 0.7.6. WoW: Forever addon: PoleSim's simulated, level-banded gear weights in game. Item scores with a
"how?" breakdown, upgrade arrows, the best quest reward, and a gear planner window with where to get
each upgrade. In testing.

**Download:** the latest zip is on the [Releases page](https://github.com/petllama/polesim-gear/releases/latest).
Unzip it into `World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns/` (a new install needs a game restart; an
update needs `/reload`, unless its notes say it adds files: 0.7.2 adds none; updating from before 0.7.0,
restart the game).

**Supported classes and specs** (weights from PoleSim's simulation of each spec leveling):

| Class   | Specs with weights                      | Default spec* |
|---------|-----------------------------------------|---------------|
| Priest  | Shadow, Holy, Discipline                | Shadow        |
| Mage    | Frost, Fire, Arcane                     | Frost         |
| Warlock | Affliction, Demonology, Destruction     | Affliction    |
| Warrior | Arms, Fury, Protection†                 | Arms          |
| Rogue   | Assassination, Combat, Subtlety         | Combat        |
| Hunter  | Beast Mastery, Marksmanship, Survival   | Beast Mastery |
| Druid   | Balance, Feral, Restoration             | Balance       |
| Shaman  | Elemental, Enhancement, Restoration     | Elemental     |
| Paladin | Holy, Retribution, Protection†          | Holy          |

\* The spec used before you have spent any talent points (below level 10). Druids, shamans and paladins
start on their caster spec (the website's import starts them on Feral, Enhancement and Retribution).
You can pick another in Settings (or `/psg spec`) at any time.

† Tanks (since 0.7.1): Protection weights are less certain than the others: Stamina, armor and
avoidance rest on the least validated parts of PoleSim's model (the Weights tab says so). Prot Paladin:
block is a damage stat from 42 (Holy Shield, Reckoning), faster weapons score better until ~55, and the
level-60 weapon speed weight is set to neutral (PoleSim's sim and wowsims/forever disagree on it). Tanks
are planned with a one-hander and a shield.

**Weapons (melee classes and hunters, 0.7.0).** A weapon scores its DPS and its **speed**: at the same
DPS, abilities that add weapon damage hit harder with a slow weapon, so Arms, Retribution and
Enhancement value slow two-handers, Combat and Subtlety a slow main hand, and most dual wielders a fast
off hand. Speed counts against the weapon PoleSim fought with at your level (the "reference"; the
website's upgrade finder names it), at most 0.6 s either way, the range PoleSim checked; Shift on a
tooltip shows the speed part. Off-hand weapons have their own DPS and speed weights. Assassination and
Subtlety need a **dagger in the main hand** (Backstab, Ambush, Mutilate) and hunters a **bow, gun or
crossbow** (not thrown): other weapons are never an upgrade there and the tooltip says why. Dual wield
(rogues from 10, Fury warriors from 20, as PoleSim plays them): a one-hander compares in the hand where
it gains most, and a set never plans one item in both hands. Feral druids score weapon DPS only (the paw
ignores the weapon's speed).

**Healer specs** (Restoration, Holy, Discipline) use leveling weights: what kills mobs fastest while
questing (Holy paladins and Restoration shamans melee a lot while leveling, so Strength and weapon DPS
count), not dungeon healing.

Druid, Shaman and Paladin relics (idols, totems, librams) are scored by their stats only; their effects
(e.g. "Increases the damage of your Moonfire spell") are not, so they score 0 today. PoleSim plays Druid,
Shaman and Paladin (and Arms warriors, hunters' melee weapon and Fury warriors below 20) with a
two-hander, so the window suggests two-handers for them (no off hand list); tooltips still compare a
shield or off hand with yours.

**Requires Polestar** (the addon reads item sources and quests from Polestar's Codex): install and
enable Polestar first, or PoleSim Gear does not load.

Scores equal the PoleSim website's armory for the same item, spec and level
(https://polesim.petllama.com/#armory): the data in `PoleSimGear/Data` is generated by
`forever/tools/gen_addon_data.py` in the PoleSim repository (not public).

**Releases are automatic:** pushing a new `## Version` in `PoleSimGear/PoleSimGear.toc` to `main` runs
the tests and publishes release `v<version>` with the zip (`.github/workflows/release.yml`); its notes
are that version's "**X over Y:**" paragraph in `docs/CHECKLIST.md` (`tools/release-notes.sh`).

## The window

`/psg`, the addon compartment button or the minimap button opens and closes it (Escape and the X close
it too; drag the title bar to move it, the grip at its bottom-right corner to resize it (780x500 up to
the screen; the place and size are kept per account), drag the minimap button around the minimap's edge). The header
shows your level and spec, your gear score (the sum of what you wear), the gear plan's progress and the
look ahead (upgrades up to 5 levels ahead). On the left: the Sources checkboxes (Quests, Dungeons,
Crafting, Vendor, BoE / World; kept per character; green world drops count as BoE / World even when
seen in a dungeon, Dungeons keep boss drops and blue+ items) and what to show (All gear, Upgrades only,
Wishlist). At the bottom: your equipped gear, with a dot on each slot that has a plan (green: a suggested
upgrade, gold: your pick; hover a slot for the item and its gain), and the stat weights in use.

- **Gear Plan:** one row per slot: your pick, else the suggestion: the best upgrade from an upcoming
  quest of your Polestar guide within 2 levels ("near quest", with the best overall's "BiS +N"; Settings
  can turn this off), else the best upgrade, with its gain and source. Click a
  row for that slot's upgrades; right-click: back to the suggestion, wishlist. Hover: the game's item tooltip (with
  its random suffix), then the "how?"; Shift compares with what you wear.
- **Slot Upgrades:** every upgrade of one slot, with its level, source, "in 12 steps" for a quest one
  of your open Polestar guides turns in ahead of you ("The Barrens, in 30 steps" for another tab,
  "next guide: X" for the guide yours goes on to; the item's tooltip says it too) and "needs: X first"; **Set as plan** makes one the slot's pick (per spec); "Show all (N)"
  when the list was trimmed. The star adds an item to your wishlist (in every list).
- **Sources:** where to go: dungeons, quest zones, professions, vendors and world drops, each with its
  upgrades; **Waypoint** sets Polestar's arrow to a quest giver, a dungeon entrance or a vendor
  (through Polestar's Nav API).
- **Weights:** your band's weights (step through the other bands) and the spec switch.
- **Settings:** minimap button, tooltip score line, upgrade arrows, the quest reward mark, preferring upcoming guide quests, the look
  ahead a new character starts with, the equip prompt (on/off, minimum gain, the never list),
  Polestar's gear-score question, and the spec (Automatic, or one of your class's specs with weights).

## Equip prompt

New gear that reaches your bags (loot, a quest reward, mail, a purchase, a trade) and is an upgrade you
can wear now asks once to be equipped: a small card directly above Polestar's guide window (under the
minimap when that window is hidden; drag it there to move it) with the item, its slot and the gain in
green, **Equip** and **Not now**. Several upgrades queue ("1 of 3"). Hover the icon for the item's
tooltip. Equip puts it in the slot it replaces (the weaker ring or trinket); an item that binds when worn
still brings up the game's own confirmation. In combat the button says "After combat" and comes back when
the fight ends. Right-click the card: **Never for this item** (kept for the account; Settings > Clear
forgets the list). Items already in your bags at login, moved between bags or just taken off never ask.
Settings: "Ask to equip upgrades I pick up" (on by default) and "Minimum gain to ask to equip" (any, 1%,
3%, 5%). While Polestar's own Gear module is on, its "Wear it" banner asks instead and this one stays
quiet. `/psg ask` asks about every upgrade in your bags now.

## Auction house upgrades

At the auction house a **PoleSim upgrades** button sits just right of the auction house's top-right
corner; it shows or hides the panel below it (kept between sessions). **Scan for upgrades** runs a few
browse searches, one at a time and as fast as the auction house allows (a priest or mage: 6, cloth,
neck/rings/trinkets/off hands and cloaks, then one per weapon type; more for classes with more armor
types (leather, mail from 40 for a shaman, plate from 40 for a paladin, shields) and weapon types; usable items, common to epic, up to
your level + the window's look ahead); **Cancel** stops it. Each item found is scored like the window
(against what you wear, rings and trinkets against the weaker one, weapons as one set: under a staff a
one-hander counts with the best off hand for sale) and listed when it is an upgrade: icon, name with its
suffix, slot, **lvl N** in gold for an item above your level (scored with that level's weights), gain,
the cheapest price, and gain per gold. Sort by **Gain**, **Value** (gain per gold) or **Price**; the
**Slot** dropdown beside them shows one slot only (**All slots** by default; it lists the slots found with
their counts, e.g. "Rings (12)", keeps your choice while the auction house stays open and goes back to
All slots when it closes). Hover a
row for the game's tooltip with PoleSim's lines and the price; **click it to see its listings** in the
auction house, cheapest first. Buying is yours: PoleSim Gear never bids or buys.

While the auction house is open after a scan, the Gear Plan says **AH: <price>** on a plan item for
sale, and an equipped slot's tooltip names a scanned item that beats the plan (**AH: <item> +N
(<price>)**). Prices are for the session only: they go when the auction house closes. The auction
house's own list shows the scan's last search afterwards (search again as usual). If the browse list
gives one row for every suffix of an item ("... of the Owl", "of the Bear"), the row is scored with its
best suffix and says "any suffix"; its listings show each one's suffix (with the upgrade arrow).

## Recording for PoleSim (opt-in)

PoleSim Gear can record how a character levels so PoleSim's model (fight lengths, kills per hour, rest,
regen, casts per kill) can be checked against real play. It is **off until you say yes**: on the first
load of a version with the recorder a PoleSim-styled dialog asks **"Help improve PoleSim's gear
weights?"** once for the account (**Yes, record** / **No thanks**); Settings > "Record leveling data for
PoleSim" changes the answer any time. Data is kept per character in your SavedVariables
(`PoleSimGearCharDB`), about the last 20 hours of active play.

**Recorded** (nothing named):
- a character sheet at login and at every level-up: level, class, race, base and effective Strength,
  Agility, Stamina, Intellect, Spirit, max health and mana, armor, attack power, ranged attack power, spell
  power per school, bonus healing, melee/ranged/spell crit, hit, spell hit, dodge/parry/block, the game's
  mana regen out of combat and while casting, weapon damage and speed, worn item ids (with suffix and
  enchant ids), talents (node and rank);
- each pull: when it started and ended (seconds into the session), how many mobs fought you, each mob's
  creature id, level, creature type and whether it uses mana, your casts per spell, kills and the XP each
  paid, the gap since the previous pull;
- meals (Food/Drink: how long you sat, the aura's length, whether a pull interrupted it), deaths (level,
  the mobs and their levels, the fight so far, time until alive and until the next pull), XP and rested
  at the session's start, each level-up and the session's end, and pauses over 2 minutes (AFK flag, or no
  combat, movement or casting) so only active play counts.

**Never recorded:** names (yours, your realm, guild, party, other players or mobs), chat, gold, map
position, health or mana values.

**Uploading:** every 2 hours of active recorded play, at a calm moment (out of combat, not in an
instance, not casting, no other PoleSim popup, not at the auction house, mail, a vendor or a trainer) a
card asks **"2 h of leveling recorded. Upload it?"**: **Copy export**, **Later** (asks again after an hour
of play) or **Stop asking** (recording goes on; Settings > "Ask me to upload every 2 h of play" turns it
back on). `/psg export` (or Settings > Export now) opens the export any time: one line of text, already
highlighted: **Ctrl+C, then paste at polesim.petllama.com/upload**. A big export comes in numbered parts
(Next). After sending, the website shows a 6-character code: click **I uploaded it** and type it in; only
then are the uploaded records removed from your SavedVariables (nothing is deleted otherwise, apart from
the 20-hour cap). A 4-hour export is about 4-5 KB. `/psg record` prints the state.

The export is compressed with LibDeflate (zlib licence, bundled in `PoleSimGear/Libs/LibDeflate`, loaded
through LibStub, public domain, `Libs/LibStub`). The recorder adds new files: **a full game restart** is
needed the first time (not just `/reload`).

## Commands

- `/psg`: open or close the window.
- `/psg spec`: the spec in use and your talent points; `/psg spec auto` or `/psg spec <name>` (e.g.
  `shadow`, `frost`, `balance`) chooses it (only specs with weights). Automatic = the talent tree with
  the most points; a tie goes as on the website's import, to the spec name later in the alphabet (Frost
  over Fire over Arcane, Shadow over Holy over Discipline, Destruction over Demonology over Affliction,
  Restoration over Feral over Balance, Restoration over Enhancement over Elemental, Retribution over
  Protection over Holy, Protection over Fury over Arms, Subtlety over Combat over Assassination,
  Survival over Marksmanship over Beast Mastery); no points yet = Shadow for a priest, Frost for a mage,
  Affliction for a warlock, Arms for a warrior, Combat for a rogue, Beast Mastery for a hunter, Balance
  for a druid, Elemental for a shaman, Holy for a paladin.
- `/psg minimap`: hide/show the minimap button (saved for the account).
- `/psg ask`: ask to equip every upgrade in your bags now (the equip prompt, even for items there at login).
- `/psg polestar`: ask again whether to turn off Polestar's own gear scores (forgets the earlier answer).
- `/psg drift` (or `/psg drift <itemID>`): compares PoleSim's shipped stats with the game's live stats for
  everything you wear and carry (or one item) and lists the differences (`spi 4 -> 6`: shipped -> live),
  e.g. after a Blizzard hotfix; items the game has not loaded yet are requested: run it again.
- `/psg export`: the recorder's copy-paste export (Recording for PoleSim); `/psg record`: recording on/off,
  hours recorded and since the last upload.

## Development

- Tests: `luajit tests/run.lua` and `lua5.1 tests/run.lua` (LuaJIT = Lua 5.1 + bit, as in the client).
- Full load against the installed Polestar's real Codex (local only): `luajit tools/fullload.lua
  [level[:class[/spec]] ...]` (e.g. `18:mage/frost`; `TOP=5` prints the Gear Plan's first rows) opens
  every tab, list and action and reports errors, build time and memory; its auction house pass runs a
  scan against a fake browse server built from the shipped items and prints the top 5 by gain and by
  value.
- Install into the game: `tools/install.sh`. Package: `tools/package.sh`.
- Recorder export fixture for PoleSim's website tests: `luajit tools/record_fixture.lua DIR [seconds]`
  writes `export.txt`, `summary.json` and `code.txt` (copy them to PoleSim's
  `forever/web/tests/fixtures/record/`). The fullload run also records 200 pulls with recording on and
  prints the handler cost per event and the export size.
- In-game checks: `docs/CHECKLIST.md`.
