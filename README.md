# PoleSim Gear

Version 0.4.1. WoW: Forever addon: PoleSim's simulated, level-banded gear weights in game. Item scores with a
"how?" breakdown, upgrade arrows, the best quest reward, and a gear planner window with where to get
each upgrade. In testing.

**Download:** the latest zip is on the [Releases page](https://github.com/petllama/polesim-gear/releases/latest).
Unzip it into `World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns/` (a new install needs a game restart; an
update needs `/reload`).

**Supports Priest and Mage** (Shadow/Holy/Discipline; Frost/Fire/Arcane). Other classes get one chat
line ("<class> isn't supported yet") and nothing else.

**Requires Polestar** (the addon reads item sources and quests from Polestar's Codex): install and
enable Polestar first, or PoleSim Gear does not load.

Scores equal the PoleSim website's armory for the same item, spec and level
(https://polesim.petllama.com/#armory): the data in `PoleSimGear/Data` is generated by
`forever/tools/gen_addon_data.py` in the PoleSim repository (not public).

**Releases are automatic:** pushing a new `## Version` in `PoleSimGear/PoleSimGear.toc` to `main` runs
the tests and publishes release `v<version>` with the zip (`.github/workflows/release.yml`); its notes
are that version's "**X over Y:**" paragraph in `docs/CHECKLIST.md` (`tools/release-notes.sh`).

## The window

`/psg`, the addon compartment button or the minimap button opens and closes it (Escape and the X close
it too; drag the title bar to move it, the grip at its bottom-right corner to resize it (780x500 up to
the screen; the place and size are kept per account), drag the minimap button around the minimap's edge). The header
shows your level and spec, your gear score (the sum of what you wear), the gear plan's progress and the
look ahead (upgrades up to 5 levels ahead). On the left: the Sources checkboxes (Quests, Dungeons,
Crafting, Vendor, BoE / World; kept per character; green world drops count as BoE / World even when
seen in a dungeon, Dungeons keep boss drops and blue+ items) and what to show (All gear, Upgrades only,
Wishlist). At the bottom: your equipped gear, with a dot on each slot that has a plan (green: a suggested
upgrade, gold: your pick; hover a slot for the item and its gain), and the stat weights in use.

- **Gear Plan:** one row per slot: your pick, else the suggestion: the best upgrade from an upcoming
  quest of your Polestar guide within 2 levels ("near quest", with the best overall's "BiS +N"; Settings
  can turn this off), else the best upgrade, with its gain and source. Click a
  row for that slot's upgrades; right-click: back to the suggestion, wishlist. Hover: the game's item tooltip (with
  its random suffix), then the "how?"; Shift compares with what you wear.
- **Slot Upgrades:** every upgrade of one slot, with its level, source, "in 12 steps" for a quest one
  of your open Polestar guides turns in ahead of you ("The Barrens, in 30 steps" for another tab,
  "next guide: X" for the guide yours goes on to; the item's tooltip says it too) and "needs: X first"; **Set as plan** makes one the slot's pick (per spec); "Show all (N)"
  when the list was trimmed. The star adds an item to your wishlist (in every list).
- **Sources:** where to go: dungeons, quest zones, professions, vendors and world drops, each with its
  upgrades; **Waypoint** sets Polestar's arrow to a quest giver, a dungeon entrance or a vendor
  (through Polestar's Nav API).
- **Weights:** your band's weights (step through the other bands) and the spec switch.
- **Settings:** minimap button, tooltip score line, upgrade arrows, the quest reward mark, preferring upcoming guide quests, the look
  ahead a new character starts with, and Polestar's gear-score question.

## Auction house upgrades

At the auction house a **PoleSim upgrades** button sits just right of the auction house's top-right
corner; it shows or hides the panel below it (kept between sessions). **Scan for upgrades** runs a few
browse searches, one at a time and as fast as the auction house allows (a priest or mage: 6, cloth,
neck/rings/trinkets/off hands and cloaks, then one per weapon type; usable items, common to epic, up to
your level + the window's look ahead); **Cancel** stops it. Each item found is scored like the window
(against what you wear, rings and trinkets against the weaker one, weapons as one set: under a staff a
one-hander counts with the best off hand for sale) and listed when it is an upgrade: icon, name with its
suffix, slot, **lvl N** in gold for an item above your level (scored with that level's weights), gain,
the cheapest price, and gain per gold. Sort by **Gain**, **Value** (gain per gold) or **Price**. Hover a
row for the game's tooltip with PoleSim's lines and the price; **click it to see its listings** in the
auction house, cheapest first. Buying is yours: PoleSim Gear never bids or buys.

While the auction house is open after a scan, the Gear Plan says **AH: <price>** on a plan item for
sale, and an equipped slot's tooltip names a scanned item that beats the plan (**AH: <item> +N
(<price>)**). Prices are for the session only: they go when the auction house closes. The auction
house's own list shows the scan's last search afterwards (search again as usual). If the browse list
gives one row for every suffix of an item ("... of the Owl", "of the Bear"), the row is scored with its
best suffix and says "any suffix"; its listings show each one's suffix (with the upgrade arrow).

## Commands

- `/psg`: open or close the window.
- `/psg spec`: the spec in use and your talent points; `/psg spec auto` or `/psg spec <name>` (e.g.
  `shadow`, `frost`) chooses it. Automatic = the talent tree with the most points (a tie goes as on the
  website's import: Frost over Fire over Arcane, Shadow over Holy over Discipline); no points yet =
  Shadow for a priest, Frost for a mage.
- `/psg minimap`: hide/show the minimap button (saved for the account).
- `/psg polestar`: ask again whether to turn off Polestar's own gear scores (forgets the earlier answer).

## Development

- Tests: `luajit tests/run.lua` and `lua5.1 tests/run.lua` (LuaJIT = Lua 5.1 + bit, as in the client).
- Full load against the installed Polestar's real Codex (local only): `luajit tools/fullload.lua
  [level[:class[/spec]] ...]` (e.g. `18:mage/frost`; `TOP=5` prints the Gear Plan's first rows) opens
  every tab, list and action and reports errors, build time and memory; its auction house pass runs a
  scan against a fake browse server built from the shipped items and prints the top 5 by gain and by
  value.
- Install into the game: `tools/install.sh`. Package: `tools/package.sh`.
- In-game checks: `docs/CHECKLIST.md`.
